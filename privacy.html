Privacy Policy

CH Encyclopedia

How CH Encyclopedia handles data and local favorites.

Last updated: August 31, 2026

Short version: CH Encyclopedia does not require an account and currently does not intentionally collect names, email addresses, Discord identities, messages, or payment information. Favorites are stored locally on your device.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how CH Encyclopedia (“the Application,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information when you use the website or Discord Activity.

2. Information We Intentionally Collect

At this time, the Application does not require user registration and does not intentionally collect or store personal information such as your name, email address, Discord username, Discord user ID, messages, contacts, payment details, or precise location.

3. Favorites and Local Storage

If you use the Favorites feature, the identifiers of the items or mobs you save are stored locally in your browser or Discord Activity storage using localStorage. This information remains on that device/browser environment unless you clear it.

Favorites are not currently synchronized to a server or tied to a user account.

4. Discord Activity

When the Application is launched as a Discord Activity, it initializes Discord’s Embedded App SDK so the Activity can run inside Discord. The current version does not request OAuth authentication or privileged Discord user, guild-member, message, presence, contact, or payment scopes.

Discord may independently process technical or account information when you use Discord. That processing is governed by Discord’s own Privacy Policy and terms.

5. Hosting and Technical Logs

The Application is hosted using GitHub Pages and may use third-party content-delivery infrastructure. Those providers may automatically process ordinary technical information such as IP address, browser type, request timestamps, and network logs as part of operating their services. We do not control those provider logs.

6. Cookies

The Application does not currently set advertising or tracking cookies. Third-party platforms used to host or embed the Application may have their own storage or cookie practices.

7. Analytics and Advertising

The Application currently does not use third-party advertising trackers or behavioral analytics. If analytics or advertising are added later, this Privacy Policy will be updated before or when those changes are deployed.

8. Data Sharing

Because we do not currently operate a user-account database or intentionally collect personal information through the Application, we do not sell personal data and do not provide user data to advertisers.

9. Children

The Application is a game-reference utility and is not designed to collect personal information from children. Users should not submit personal information through any future feedback or contact feature unless appropriate and permitted by applicable law.

10. Data Retention

Favorites remain locally stored until you remove them, clear the Application’s storage, clear your browser data, or the platform removes that storage. We do not currently maintain a server-side copy of favorites.

11. Your Choices

You can remove saved favorites through the Application or by clearing browser/Activity storage. You may also contact us with privacy questions at the email below.

12. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as the Application changes. If we later add accounts, cloud-synced favorites, analytics, feedback forms, or other features that process additional information, this policy will be revised accordingly.

13. Contact

Privacy questions can be sent to celtichero2026@gmail.com.
